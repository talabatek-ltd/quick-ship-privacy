Account Deletion

App: QuickShip

At QuickShip we respect our users' privacy and are committed to giving you full control over your data. You can delete your account at any time by following the steps below.

Steps to delete your account:

1. Open the QuickShip app and sign in to your account.

2. Go to the Profile section (Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar).

3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".

4. Confirm your request:
   - If you signed in with email and password: enter your password when prompted, then confirm deletion.
   - If you signed in with Google: confirm deletion, then sign in with Google again when prompted to verify your identity.

5. Confirm the deletion in the dialog to complete the process permanently.

Data that will be deleted:

When you delete your account, all data associated with it will be removed, including:

- Basic account information (name, email, phone)
- Order history and app usage data
- User settings and preferences
- Any data stored in your user profile

Data that may be retained:

Some data may be retained for a limited period when required for legal, regulatory, or security purposes only. Such data will be permanently deleted after the required retention period ends.

Contact:

For any questions regarding account deletion or data management, you can contact us through the support options available within the app.
