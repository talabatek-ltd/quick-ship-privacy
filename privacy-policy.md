# Privacy Policy – QuickShip

**Last updated:** January 2025

QuickShip ("we", "our", or "the app") is a delivery and shipping app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the QuickShip application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, phone number, and authentication credentials (when you sign up or sign in with email/password or Google).
- **Order and delivery data:** Information you provide when creating or tracking orders (e.g. sender/receiver details, addresses, notes).
- **Usage data:** How you use the app (e.g. features used, order history) to improve the service.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Process and manage your delivery orders.
- Allow you to track orders and view order history.
- Improve the app and user experience.
- Comply with applicable laws and respond to legal requests.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored using **Firebase** (Google), including **Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore**.
- We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app uses:

- **Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore) – [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Sign-In** – for optional sign-in with your Google account

Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

- You can **access and update** your profile (name, phone) from the Profile and Edit Profile screens.
- You can **delete your account** at any time from the Profile screen (see **Account Deletion** below). When you delete your account, we delete your account data as described in our account deletion instructions.
- You can **sign out** at any time from the Profile screen.

---

## 6. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and associated data at any time. For step-by-step instructions and details on what data is deleted, please see the document **deleteAccount.txt** (or the in-app account deletion section).

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

QuickShip is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

---

## 9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, account deletion, or your data, please contact us through the support or contact options provided in the app or on the app’s store listing.
